**County-Wise Analysis of COPD, Heart Disease, Obesity, and Income**
---
•	Created and presented a poster consisting of graphs and maps, using ggplot2 and Tableau, to investigate what and where high prevalence of disease and low income were the most prominent in the U.S.  
•	Used tidyverse packages to import and clean raw datasets from the CDC and U.S. Census Bureau  
•	Discovered that the Southeast region, New Mexico, Maine, and Idaho needed aid because they were hit the hardest with disease and low income
